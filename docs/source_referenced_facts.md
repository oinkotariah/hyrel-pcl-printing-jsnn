# Source-Referenced Key Facts

*Each claim paired with its exact location so you can cite it on the poster. Format: claim — (Author Year, page / Figure / Section). Page numbers are the journal page printed on the PDF. ⭐ = most likely to go on the poster.*

*Note: none of these five is the PCL+graphite composite paper, so anything about the **percolation threshold / graphite loading** must be cited to that paper (not yet in this set) — don't pin it to these.*

---

## Yuk et al. 2020 — conducting polymers (Nat. Commun. 11, 1604) — *your north star*
- ⭐ Conductivity **155 S/cm (dry), 28 S/cm (hydrogel)** — (Yuk 2020, p.4, Fig.3a).
- ⭐ **<5% conductivity change** across bending radii; still **>100 S/cm (dry) / >15 S/cm (hydrogel) after 10,000 bends** — (Yuk 2020, p.4, Fig.3b–c).
- ⭐ Printable ink window **5–7 wt%** PEDOT:PSS nanofibrils (below → spreads, above → clogs) — (Yuk 2020, p.2, Fig.1m,o).
- Young's modulus **1.5 GPa dry vs 1.1 MPa hydrogel** (soft as PDMS) — (Yuk 2020, p.5, Fig.3f).
- Resolution to a **30 µm nozzle**, up to **20 layers** high aspect ratio — (Yuk 2020, p.3, Fig.2).
- Multi-material with insulating PDMS; **9-channel neural probe, 30 µm electrodes, 50–150 kΩ @1 kHz** — (Yuk 2020, p.5, Fig.4d).
- Ink prep: cryo-freeze → lyophilize → redisperse in **water:DMSO 85:15**; then **dry 60 °C, anneal 130 °C** — (Yuk 2020, Methods, p.6–7).

## Zhang et al. 2021 — DIW of PCL/PEO (Prog. Nat. Sci. Mater. Int. 31, 180–191) — *your PCL-DIW how-to*
- ⭐ The **three DIW ink rules**: shear-thinning, clean filament, **>80% viscosity recovery** after deposition — (Zhang 2021, p.2, Intro).
- ⭐ **DCM ink recovered ~93%** (full by ~100 s); **acetone inks <80%** (44% low / 81% high conc.) → **DCM preferred** — (Zhang 2021, p.6, Sect.3.1.2).
- All inks **power-law index n < 1** (shear-thinning) — (Zhang 2021, p.5, Fig.3c–d).
- Acetone ink shear rate **~9× higher** than DCM at same flow (1702.9 vs 195.2 s⁻¹) — (Zhang 2021, p.7, Sect.3.2).
- Materials: **PCL Mn ~80,000, PEO Mn ~200,000**; PCL:PEO **1:1**; **24G nozzle** — (Zhang 2021, p.2, Sect.2.1–2.2).
- Print window: speed **2.5–12.5 mm/s**, flow **0.48–1.44 mm³/s**; best fidelity DCM @ **5 mm/s + 0.48 mm³/s** — (Zhang 2021, p.3 & p.8, Sect.2.3.3 / 3.3).
- PCL/PEO **immiscible**; raw Tm PCL **60.9 °C**, PEO **70.2 °C** — (Zhang 2021, p.8, Fig.7b).
- PEO addition raised wettability (contact angle D_PCL **74.3°** → D_PCL/PEO **37°**) — (Zhang 2021, p.10, Fig.8d).

## Teixeira et al. 2026 — parameter optimization (MRS Commun. 16, 212–218) — *your optimization playbook*
- ⭐ Best line accuracy **97.3%** at **2% agarose, 25G, 6 mm/s, 40 kPa** — (Teixeira 2026, Abstract p.1 & Results p.5).
- ⭐ **Accuracy = (1 − |A_exp − A_CAD| / A_CAD) × 100** (Eq.1) — (Teixeira 2026, p.3).
- ⭐ **Pore printability Pr = L² / 16A**; Pr = 1 perfect, <1 under-gelation, >1 over-gelation (Eq.3) — (Teixeira 2026, p.4).
- Parameters swept: speed **1–10 mm/s**, pressure **40–100 kPa**, gauge **25G/27G/30G** — (Teixeira 2026, p.3).
- **3% laponite** raised grid accuracy **60.3% → 83.5%** — (Teixeira 2026, p.5, Fig.4).
- Rule of thumb: to raise accuracy, **↑ speed & gauge, ↓ pressure** — (Teixeira 2026, p.6, Discussion).
- Method is **material-agnostic** for any shear-thinning ink — (Teixeira 2026, p.2 & Conclusions p.6).

## Liu et al. 2018 — melt-extrusion crystallinity (Macromol. Mater. Eng. 303, 1700494)
- Screw-assisted melt extrusion: **0.5 mm nozzle, 10 rpm, 110 °C → 0.1 mm/s** — (Liu 2018, p.2, Sect.2.1).
- PCL **CAPA6500, Mw 50,000**; Tm **58–60 °C**, Tg **−60 °C** — (Liu 2018, p.2, Sect.2.2).
- Spherulite size **~40 µm (pellet) → <4 µm (printed)** — (Liu 2018, p.2, Fig.2).
- Crystals **align in flow direction**; crystal volume fraction **increases down the nozzle**; driven by undercooling + shear — (Liu 2018, p.4–5, Figs.4–5).

## Woodruff & Hutmacher 2010 — PCL review (Prog. Polym. Sci. 35, 1217–1256)
- ⭐ PCL **Tg −60 °C, Tm 59–64 °C**; MW **3,000–80,000**; **crystallinity decreases as MW rises** — (Woodruff 2010, p.3, Sect.2).
- ⭐ Soluble in **chloroform/DCM**; **poor in acetone**; insoluble in alcohols — (Woodruff 2010, p.3, Sect.2).
- Degradation **2–4 years**; two-stage (hydrolysis, then intracellular below MW ~3,000) — (Woodruff 2010, p.4–5, Sect.3).
- Fabrication-method table (porosity & pore size per method) — (Woodruff 2010, Fig.9).
- *(bone background)* mPCL + **20 wt% β-TCP**; commercialized as Osteopore — (Woodruff 2010, Sect.8.2 & 10, p.24–25, 35).

## How to use this on the poster
- When you state a number, copy the parenthetical — e.g., "*…holds conductivity through 10,000 bends (Yuk 2020).*"
- In a reference list, the **index numbers** are inside each paper's own bibliography; the **journal + year + page** here is enough to find any claim fast.
- If a reviewer asks "where's that from?", you can point to the exact page/figure in seconds.
